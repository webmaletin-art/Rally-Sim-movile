## English

**Title:** Dream Racing: Rally & Drift

**Short description:** 26 cars, open world, rally, drift and a 12-stage adventure.

**Full description:**
Dream Racing is a rally, drift and open-world driving game made for your phone, with drivers who live every corner. Pick your car, tune it down to the last bolt and hit the road.

🚗 26 DETAILED CARS
Hatchbacks, demolition classics, 4x4 pick-ups, Dakar buggies, Group B rally cars, muscle cars, GT3, supercars, hypercars and 6x6 trucks. Each one looks, sounds and drives differently. Test drive any car in the shop before you buy it.

🌆 DREAM CITY AND ITS REGION
An open city to explore without limits and, around it, a huge region with more than 2,000 km of roads: ring road, highways, mountain passes, 8 cities and 25 towns with toll booths, service stations and tyre shops. GPS with distance and time, a big map, and the freedom to start from any place you choose.

🌄 ADVENTURE: THE ROAD OF DREAMS
One very long road, station to station: 12 stages through cities, countryside, mountains, tunnels, a quarry and snow. Every stage has a rival to beat.

🚛 CROSSING X
A 44 km off-road convoy of five vehicles: mud, fords, rock fields and cliff ledges. Don't fall behind, don't lose them and don't get too far ahead.

🏆 CUPS AND RACES
Four championships —Debut, National, Continental and Legend— with time trials, races against rivals, downhill runs and truck rally raid. Tarmac, dirt, rain, fog, night and sunset.

🛣 ROUTE NETWORK
1,600 km of dirt and tarmac roads, roundabouts and service stations. Drive solo, run from a rival who is chasing you, or chase them yourself.

🌀 DRIFT AND DRAG
A huge square to slide around chaining combos, and straight-line drag races.

🌌 FANTASY MAPS
Race in impossible worlds made of paper: a vortex of giant flowers with a black hole, the volcano of Mars, the Moon with the Earth in the sky and Jupiter's ring.

🔧 EASY TUNING
Engine, suspension, brakes, differential, aerodynamics, tyres, rims and paint with 24 styles. Every setting explains what happens if you raise or lower it, so you don't need to know about cars.

🎮 CONTROLS YOUR WAY
On-screen wheel, pedals or tilt steering; automatic or manual gearbox; resize and move every control.

🎬 SHOW IT OFF
Cockpit cameras with drivers, free and cinematic cameras, lens effects and a photo mode for your best shots. Radio music in the menus.

No ads. To play you create a free account with your email: your credits, cars and purchases are saved in your account (you keep them if you change phones) and, once you're in, the game also works offline. Includes optional in-app purchases. Online mode coming soon.
